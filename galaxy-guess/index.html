How to play

  1. Turn the phone sideways and hold it on your forehead, screen facing your team.
  2. Your team gives clues, or acts it out, without saying the word.
  3. Guessed it? A teammate taps the right side. Stuck? Tap the left side to pass.
  4. Get as many as you can before the timer runs out.
